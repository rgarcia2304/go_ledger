Six claims a ledger has to prove

Go Ledger makes specific guarantees about correctness. The claims are that no lost money, no double charges, no corruption under concurrent load. Below, each guarantee is tested against the real, running API. Nothing here is simulated: every button sends an actual HTTP request and shows you what came back.
